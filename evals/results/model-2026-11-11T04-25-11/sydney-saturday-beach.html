Saturday, 11:30 a.m. · Sydney

The Hour Before Lunch

It is 27° and mostly clear. The sky has not been interrupted yet, and for the next hour or so nothing is due to interrupt it.

Late morning on a clear Saturday is the part of the day with no agenda attached. Breakfast is done. Lunch has not been decided. The 12:00 reading is 28°, and the rain chance sits at 17%, with no rain forecast to fall.

This is a good hour for being outside without a plan. Walk to the shop that is slightly too far to walk to. Take the long way. Sit somewhere with a view of the sky and let it do the work.

Sunrise was at 5:45 a.m., so the sun has been up nearly six hours and is close to its highest point. Light this high is flat and bright. Shadows are short and sit right under things.

Afternoon Edition

Where the Afternoon Is Heading

It is 27° now. By 1 p.m. it reaches 29°, and it stays there until about 6 p.m.

The 23° in the forecast is the overnight low, not something you will feel at lunch. The day only goes one way from here: a couple of degrees warmer, then a long flat top through the afternoon.

The sky stays clear the whole stretch. Rain chances sit between 5% and 19% every hour, and none of the hours carries any measurable rain. Sunset is at 7:35 p.m., so the warm part of the day has room to run.

After 7 p.m. it eases to 27°, then 26° by 8 and 25° by 10. Even at night it stays mild.
