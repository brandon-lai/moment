Fog over the city this morning

It is 5° and foggy. The sun came up at 7:26 a.m., but you would not know it from the street.

Fog narrows the world. Buildings a few streets away fade first, then the far end of your own street. Sound carries oddly. Everything looks closer to grey than to any colour.

The rest of Thursday

Making the most of a clearing day

Do the indoor things while the fog holds. Save the outdoor ones for noon, when the sky is clear and it is 9°.

  1. Until 9 a.m.

    Fog. Stay in, or keep to short, familiar routes. Views are poor and nothing is worth a detour yet.

  2. 10 a.m. to noon

    Cloud thinning to clear. Good for errands, a long coffee, or whatever you would put off for a better view.

  3. Noon to 4 p.m.

    Clear and 9°, with no rain on the forecast. This is the stretch to spend outside. A walk by the river or across a park will look nothing like this morning.

  4. 4:08 p.m.

    Sunset. The light goes early in November, so the best of the daylight is the afternoon, not the evening.

A clear sky in mid-November also means the evening cools off: 8° at 6 p.m., 7° by 7. Take a layer if you are out past sunset.
