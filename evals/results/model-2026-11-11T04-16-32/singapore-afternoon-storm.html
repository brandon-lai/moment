Thursday, 3:30 p.m. · Singapore

The storm over Singapore now

Thunderstorms, 34°C. The rain stops around 7 p.m.

It is the hottest hour of the afternoon and the storm is sitting right on it. The 3 p.m. hour reads 34° with a 73% chance of rain and about 4 mm falling. The 4 p.m. hour is 33° with 87%. By 5 p.m. the chance is 94%, though the sky code softens from thunder to steady rain.

Sunset is at 6:51 p.m., so the last of the rain and the last of the light overlap. At 7 p.m. the chance drops to 2% and the sky goes to plain cloud.

Chance of rain, 2 p.m. to 8 p.m. The drop at 7 p.m. is the point.

02 · After the storm

7 p.m.

The rain chance drops from 89% at 6 p.m. to 2% at 7 p.m. That is the edge to watch.

Between 2 p.m. and 6 p.m. every hour sits at 73% or higher. At 7 p.m. the sky reads overcast, not stormy, and the temperature slips to 31°. Sunset is 6:51 p.m., so the clearing arrives just after the light goes.
