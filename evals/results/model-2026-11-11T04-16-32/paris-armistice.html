Armistice Day, and what it means today

It is a public holiday in France, and it is 11 a.m. in Paris. Overcast, 10°C, no rain in the hours ahead.

Treat today like a Sunday that landed on a Wednesday. The day off is the point. Anyone with a calendar that says "Wednesday" will be working from the wrong assumption, so check opening hours before you walk anywhere with a plan.
