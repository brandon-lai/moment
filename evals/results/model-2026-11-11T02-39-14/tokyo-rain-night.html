The dry hour after

From midnight the rain chance drops to 7%, and the temperature keeps sliding: 10° at midnight, 9° at 1 a.m., 8° by 2 a.m.

So the dry window comes with a chill. It's a few degrees colder than it is right now, and the ground will still be wet. Pavements will be slick and puddles will hold. Wear a jacket you'd actually want at 8°, and shoes that don't mind water.

It stays at 8° through about 5 a.m., then climbs to 11° by 8 a.m. The sky stays partly cloudy and the rain chance stays at 16% or less all night.
