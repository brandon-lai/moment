03

Black Consciousness Day today

Today, 20 November, is Black Consciousness Day, a public holiday across Brazil. It falls on a Friday this year, so the weekend starts tonight and the city is already in a slower gear.

The holiday honors Black history and resistance in Brazil. It is observed on the anniversary of the death of Zumbi dos Palmares, leader of the Quilombo dos Palmares. Many places close or run short hours today, so check a venue before you cross town for it.
