Wednesday, 11 November

Armistice Day, observed today

It is 11:00 a.m. in Paris, overcast, 10°C. The holiday is today, and the city is quieter for it.

Armistice Day marks the end of fighting in the First World War, which stopped at eleven in the morning on 11 November 1918. In France it is a public holiday, so many shops and offices stay shut and the pace drops.

Across the country there are ceremonies at war memorials, usually led by the local mayor. In Paris, the main one is held at the Arc de Triomphe, where the Unknown Soldier lies under the arch.

11:00 a.m. · Wednesday

A grey midday over the city

The sky is one flat sheet of cloud, and it is 10°C. No shadows, no glare. The light comes from everywhere at once.

Overcast light suits a day like this. Stone looks like stone. Zinc roofs, wet-dark pavements and bare plane trees all sit in the same quiet range of greys, and nothing competes for your attention.

It warms slowly. 11°C at noon, 12°C by one, a high of 13°C around three. Rain chances stay low all day, never above 20%. Coat weather, not umbrella weather.

Sunset is at 5:17 p.m., so the grey dims early.

11:00 a.m. onward

The rest of the day ahead

Overcast all the way through, between 7° and 13°. The sky does not change its mind.
